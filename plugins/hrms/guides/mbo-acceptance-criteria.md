What every quarterly MBO must meet before it is agreed. The MBO coach checks
each MBO it suggests against this list, and so should the person and their
mentor.

## The five tests (SMART)

### Specific
One clear outcome, in plain words, that someone else could check.

- Good: *Ship the invoice export API for the billing rewrite.*
- Not acceptable: *Improve code quality.* (What would be different, and where?)

### Measurable
The KPI is a number or a date. "Done" can be checked without opinion.

- Good: *Reduce open P1 bugs on the billing module from 12 to 3 or fewer.*
- Not acceptable: *Be more proactive.*

### Achievable
Within the person's own control and capacity this quarter, given their
project work, leave and skills. A target that depends on another team's
decision is not theirs to own.

- Good: *Write and run the load test for the invoice API.*
- Not acceptable: *Get the client to sign the next phase.* (Not in their control.)

### Relevant
Tied to their project's needs this quarter and to what is expected at their
designation (see *Role expectations by designation*).

- Good: for a Senior Software Engineer, *Review every billing pull request
  within one working day.*
- Not acceptable: an objective unrelated to their project or role.

### Time-bound
Every date falls inside the quarter being set. The quarters run on the
HRMS's cut-off (Q1: 1 Apr – 15 Jun, Q2: 16 Jun – 15 Sep, Q3: 16 Sep – 15 Dec,
Q4: 16 Dec – 15 Mar).

- Good: *by 30 Nov 2026*.
- Not acceptable: *by next year*, or no date.

## Rules for the whole set

- **Two to three MBOs** a quarter.
- **Weightages total exactly 100.** No single MBO below 10.
- **At least one MBO is about growth**: a skill, a responsibility or a
  certification the person is building towards.
- **No duplicate of last quarter** unless it was not met and is still needed;
  then say so in the objective.
- **Agreed with the mentor** before it is submitted in the HRMS.
