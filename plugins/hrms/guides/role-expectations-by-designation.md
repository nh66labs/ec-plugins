What is expected of a person at each designation in a quarter. The MBO coach
ties each MBO it suggests to the expectations for the person's designation.

## Software Engineer

- **Delivery:** completes the tasks assigned to them in each sprint, with tests.
- **Quality:** their changes pass review with few rounds; fixes their own bugs
  quickly.
- **Learning:** builds depth in one part of the project's codebase.
- **Team:** asks for help early; shares what they learn.

## Senior Software Engineer

- **Delivery:** owns a feature end to end, from design to release.
- **Quality:** reviews others' work promptly and raises the team's standards.
- **Mentoring:** guides at least one junior engineer.
- **Ownership:** spots risks in their area and raises them before they become
  delays.

## Tech Lead

- **Delivery:** plans the team's work for the quarter and keeps it on track.
- **Design:** makes and records the technical decisions for the project.
- **People:** mentors the team and gives feedback each quarter.
- **Stakeholders:** keeps the project manager and client informed of risks.
